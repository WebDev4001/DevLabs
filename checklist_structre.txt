    DevLabs Website Structure


1. Home Page (Landing Page)
Header:
● Logo (DevLabs)
● Navigation Menu (Home | About | Events | Domains | Contact)
Hero Section:
● Catchy tagline about DevLabs (e.g., "Empowering Innovators, Building the Future").
● Background image or illustration related to tech development.
● "Join Us" CTA button (leading to the About section or a signup form).
About DevLabs (Short Overview):
● A brief introduction about the community and its mission.
● Mention of the domains covered (Web Dev, Mobile, ML/AI, Drone/IoT).
Quick Links / Highlights:
● Recent Events
● Community Stats (Number of Members, Projects, etc.)
● Testimonials (Optional)
Footer:
● Social Media Links
● Copyright Information




2. About Page
Detailed Overview of DevLabs
● History and Purpose
● Vision & Mission
Domains We Cover:
● Web Development
● Mobile App Development
● Machine Learning & AI
● Drone & IoT Systems
Core Team:
● Details about the founding members and key organizers
Community Members:
● Feature some active members with testimonials
How to Join Us:
● Membership benefits
● Steps to become a member
Contact Information:
● Contact Form: Name, Email, Message
● Social Media Links
● Location (if required)
● Email & Other Contact Info








3. Events Page
Upcoming Events:
● Event Name, Date, Description, Registration Link
Past Events:
● Event Highlights, Photos, and Achievements
Workshops & Hackathons:
● List of tech-related events organized by DevLabs
Additional Features
Gallery Section ( Images from events, projects, and meetups)
